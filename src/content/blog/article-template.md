Write a short opening that directly answers the reader’s question. Avoid
generic introductions and unsupported health claims.

## Give the first useful answer

Explain the core idea clearly. Link to a primary, authoritative source when a
claim involves health or a numerical recommendation.

## Turn the answer into a practical routine

Use concrete steps, examples, and limitations.

### Keep the first step small

Explain one action the reader can try today.

### Adjust reminders to real life

Explain how context changes the recommendation.

## What to remember

Summarize the useful conclusion without repeating the entire article.
